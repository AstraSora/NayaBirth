Quick update -- I went ahead and made the changes we discussed to the birth plan. Here's a rundown of what changed and why:

Removed the episiotomy question entirely. Per your feedback, that's a clinical decision made in the moment by the provider, not something we should be presenting as a patient preference. It was creating exactly the kind of false autonomy discussion you flagged. Patients who already answered it won't see any errors -- the app just skips over data for questions that no longer exist.

Expanded the labor environment section with the comfort and anxiety-reducing options from the email thread. The full list now includes dim lighting, fairy lights/string lights, music/playlist, quiet atmosphere, minimal interruptions, aromatherapy/aromatic diffuser, battery-operated candles, cold compresses, and a birth ball. I marked the new items that UCI would need to check on (fairy lights, candles) as "ask your care team" so patients know those aren't guaranteed. Cold compresses and the birth ball are marked as available since those should be straightforward.

Added a follow-up text box for music preferences. If a patient selects "Music/playlist" from the environment options, a text field pops up asking what songs, playlists, or genres they'd like. If they don't pick music, they never see it. Figured this gives the care team something actionable instead of just knowing the patient wants music playing.

Reworded the newborn procedures question. It used to say "please indicate any you'd like to discuss or decline" which implied these standard-of-care procedures were optional. Changed it to "which would you like more information about?" Same options, same data -- just framing it correctly so we're offering education rather than suggesting patients can opt out of things like vitamin K or the hearing screening.

No other files needed to change. The app pulls everything from a single data file, so the UI, review page, and PDF printout all pick up the updates automatically.

Happy to walk through any of this on a call if it'd be easier, or make adjustments if anything doesn't match what you had in mind.
